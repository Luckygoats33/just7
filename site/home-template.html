Quick answers

Asked a lot.

More in the full FAQ, or tap Ask Just.

Does it clean as well as liquid detergent?

Just 7 is made for everyday loads. We'll publish independent cleaning test results before launch rather than make claims early. For heavy food and protein stains, pretreat first.

Does it dissolve in cold water?

It's designed to dissolve in warm or cold water, and we're running a cold-water test to show it. For very cold washes, tear the sheet in half and don't overfill the drum.

Is it OK for homes with cats and dogs?

We made it with pets in mind: no fragrance, no essential oils, nothing from animals. Like any detergent it isn't food, so keep the pouch sealed and out of reach.

How does the subscription work?

Pick every 4, 8 or 12 weeks. Each pouch is $15 on every delivery, with free shipping. Skip or cancel anytime before it ships.

What if I don't like it?

You have 90 days. Email us and we'll refund the order. Keep the pouch, no return needed.
